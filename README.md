## 🚀 What I'm working on

### 🥗 DNC Clinic App

I'm developing a complete digital ecosystem for **Draco Nutrition Clinic**, focused on improving the experience for both patients and nutrition professionals.

The platform includes:

* 👤 Patient registration and authentication
* 📋 Initial clinical evaluation
* 📊 Monthly follow-ups
* 📸 Evolution photo gallery
* 📈 Patient progress tracking
* 🩺 Clinical records
* 🍎 Personalized nutrition plans
* 🏋️ Training plans
* 📚 Recipes and recommendations
* 📊 Clinical dashboards and KPIs
* 🔐 Role-based access for administrators and patients

This project is helping me apply software development to a real business and clinical environment.
